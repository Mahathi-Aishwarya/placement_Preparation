# Placement Diagnostic Test (Baseline)

**Time:** 25 minutes, set a timer. **Rules:** pen and paper only, no calculator, no phone, no searching.
Mark a question you are unsure about with `?`, and skip any question that takes more than 90 seconds. Come back to it at the end if time remains.

**How to submit:** reply to me in chat in this format:

```
1c 2a 3d ... 20b
Time taken: 24 min
Skipped: 7, 12
Guessed: 5, 9
```

I'll grade it, show you which topics are weak, and tell you where to start. Don't worry about the score. This test only shows us the starting line.

## Quantitative (1-8)

**1.** A price is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?
a) no change  b) 2% decrease  c) 4% increase  d) 4% decrease

**2.** A can finish a job in 12 days and B can finish it in 18 days. Working together, how many days will they take?
a) 6  b) 7.5  c) 7.2  d) 8

**3.** A train 150 m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?
a) 36  b) 30  c) 40  d) 45

**4.** The average of 5 numbers is 24. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining four is 22. What is the number removed?
a) 28  b) 32  c) 30  d) 34

**5.** The ages of A and B are in the ratio 3:5. After 6 years, the ratio will be 2:3. What is B's present age?
a) 25  b) 35  c) 30  d) 40

**6.** What is the compound interest on Rs. 10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years?
a) Rs. 2,000  b) Rs. 2,200  c) Rs. 2,500  d) Rs. 2,100

**7.** How many 3-digit numbers are divisible by 7?
a) 127  b) 128  c) 129  d) 130

**8.** Two fair dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum is 8?
a) 5/36  b) 1/6  c) 1/9  d) 7/36

## Logical reasoning (9-14)

**9.** Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
a) 40  b) 44  c) 42  d) 46

**10.** In a certain code, COMPUTER is written as DPNQVUFS. How is CLOUD written in that code?
a) DNPVE  b) DMPVE  c) BKNTC  d) DMQVE

**11.** Pointing to a man, a woman says, "He is the son of my mother's only brother." How is the man related to the woman?
a) brother  b) uncle  c) nephew  d) cousin

**12.** Ravi walks 10 m north, turns right and walks 5 m, then turns right again and walks 10 m. How far is he from his starting point, and in which direction?
a) 5 m west  b) 5 m east  c) 10 m east  d) 25 m

**13.** Statements: All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are artists.
Conclusions: I. Some engineers are artists. II. Some artists are graduates.
a) only I follows  b) only II follows  c) both follow  d) neither follows

**14.** Find the odd one out: 27, 64, 100, 125
a) 27  b) 64  c) 100  d) 125

## Verbal (15-20)

**15.** The number of applicants ___ increasing every year.
a) are  b) were  c) have been  d) is

**16.** Choose the word closest in meaning to **meticulous**.
a) careless  b) rapid  c) lazy  d) careful and precise

**17.** Choose the word opposite in meaning to **scarce**.
a) rare  b) plentiful  c) costly  d) tiny

**18.** Find the part with the error: (a) She don't know / (b) the answer / (c) to the question / (d) No error

**19.** He is senior ___ me by three years.
a) than  b) from  c) to  d) with

**20.** Read the passage and answer.

> Cloud computing lets companies rent servers, storage and software over the internet instead of buying and maintaining their own hardware. This lowers upfront costs and lets them scale resources up or down as demand changes. However, it also makes them depend on the provider's reliability and security.

Which disadvantage does the passage mention?
a) high upfront cost  b) dependence on the provider  c) inability to scale  d) the need to buy hardware
